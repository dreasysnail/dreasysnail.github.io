abstract: "Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) enhances large language models (LLMs) with external knowledge but still suffers from long contexts and disjoint retrieval-generation optimization. We propose CLaRa, a framework performing embedding-based compression and joint optimization in a shared continuous space. It introduces SCP, a data synthesis framework, and trains components end-to-end via language modeling loss with a differentiable top-k estimator, achieving state-of-the-art compression and reranking performance on QA benchmarks."
