abstract: "Large Language Models (LLMs) excel at answering questions but often struggle with efficiently gathering relevant information when it is missing. We propose BED-LLM, a framework combining Bayesian Experimental Design with LLMs to enable intelligent, goal-directed information gathering. Our approach formulates the question-asking process as an adaptive experimental design problem, where the model learns to ask maximally informative questions. BED-LLM demonstrates significant improvements in information acquisition efficiency across diverse domains including medical diagnosis, troubleshooting, and general knowledge tasks."
