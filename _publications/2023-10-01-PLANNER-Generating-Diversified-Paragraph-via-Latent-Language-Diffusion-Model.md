abstract: "Autoregressive models for text sometimes generate repetitive and low-quality output because errors accumulate during generation. We propose PLANNER, which merges latent semantic diffusion with autoregressive generation to produce fluent, lengthy text while maintaining paragraph-level control. The approach combines a decoding module with a planning module that generates semantic embeddings progressively, demonstrating effectiveness on semantic generation, text completion, and summarization tasks."
