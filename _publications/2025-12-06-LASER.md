abstract: "Chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning has enabled significant improvements in language model capabilities but often leads to excessively long and inefficient outputs. We propose LASER (Length-Adaptive Shaping of Explicit Rewards), a reward shaping method that encourages models to generate concise yet accurate reasoning chains. Our approach dynamically adjusts the length penalty based on the reasoning complexity, enabling models to learn when detailed reasoning is necessary and when it can be abbreviated. Experiments show LASER achieves comparable or better accuracy while significantly reducing reasoning length across multiple benchmarks."
