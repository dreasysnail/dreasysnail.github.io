abstract: "We present Target Concrete Score Matching (TCSM), a novel training objective for discrete diffusion models. TCSM provides a general framework with broad applicability, supporting pre-training discrete diffusion models directly from data samples. Many existing discrete diffusion approaches naturally emerge as special cases of our more general TCSM framework. The framework enables fine-tuning using reward functions, preference data, and knowledge distillation from autoregressive models by estimating the concrete score of the target distribution in the original clean data space."
