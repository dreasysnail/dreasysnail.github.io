abstract: "We present ASTRA-bench, a benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents on complex, real-world scenarios. The framework incorporates time-evolving personal context with an interactive toolbox and complex user intents, generating 2,413 test scenarios across four characters. Advanced models like Claude-4.5-Opus and DeepSeek-V3.2 reveal significant performance degradation under high-complexity conditions, with argument generation emerging as the primary bottleneck."
