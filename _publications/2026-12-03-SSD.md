abstract: "We demonstrate that language models can enhance code generation performance by sampling their own outputs and fine-tuning on them—without external verifiers or teachers. Our approach, simple self-distillation (SSD), improved Qwen3-30B-Instruct performance from 42.4% to 55.3% pass@1 on LiveCodeBench v6. The method works by suppressing distractor tails where precision matters while preserving useful diversity where exploration matters, and shows consistent gains across multiple model sizes and variants."
