abstract: "We present LaDi-RL, combining latent diffusion models with reinforcement learning for test-time scaling in reasoning tasks. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art results on mathematical reasoning benchmarks: 20.5 on AIME25 and 52.7 on LCB v6 using only an 8B parameter model, with 2x faster inference compared to autoregressive approaches. Surprisingly, we find that RL training for latent reasoning does not suffer from the entropy collapse and diversity reduction commonly observed in autoregressive models, maintaining solution diversity while improving accuracy."
